KOGA — Retail ERP & point-of-sale system

A complete system for running a wholesale & retail business: sales, stock across branches, purchasing, suppliers, field sales reps, cash and reports — in a fully right-to-left Sorani Kurdish interface. I designed it, built every part of it, and use it in my own store.

Jan 2026 — PresentBuilt for and used daily at Abdulla Barza Store, QaladzeSolo: design, backend, frontend, testing, DevOps

Why I built it

Ready-made systems didn't fit how stores here actually work: a Kurdish right-to-left interface, goods sold by carton, pack or piece, field sales reps taking orders on the road, and an internet connection that can't be trusted. So I built one that does.

01

Offline-first point of sale

Sales are saved in the browser (IndexedDB) and replayed through a sync queue with idempotency keys, so a lost connection never loses or duplicates a sale. Works with barcode scanners, label and receipt printers.

02

Real retail rules

Multi-branch stock and transfers, carton › pack › piece units, weighted products, price tiers, purchase orders, supplier invoices & claims, expiry tracking, stock counts and daily cash close.

03

Field-sales module

A mobile app for sales reps: customer visits, route and order maps, returns, customer statements, debt reminders, commission & pay calculation, and Telegram alerts for new orders.

04

Every change is accounted for

EF Core interceptors write a full audit log and an append-only stock ledger; a background worker processes stock updates. Fine-grained permissions per role and per user.

05

Business insight

Revenue vs. profit, margins, customer segments, best sellers, daily reports and automatic reorder suggestions — with Excel and PDF export.

06

Tested and shipped like a team product

53 API integration suites against a real PostgreSQL database and 52 Playwright browser suites run on every pull request. Merges deploy automatically to Cloudflare Pages and Render.
